About Me
---------------
Hello! My name is <a href="https://orcid.org/0009-0006-9651-1930"><em>Junpeng Chen</em></a>. I am a  master’s graduate from Fujian University of Technology, class of 2026, supervised by Professor <a href="https://sites.google.com/view/zhenyumeng/"><em>Zhenyu Meng</em></a>. My primary research interest lies in computational intelligence, with a focus on the applications of differential evolution algorithms to power system economic dispatch and unmanned aerial vehicle path planning problems. Meanwhile, I have a strong passion for various emerging frontiers of artificial intelligence and am willing to explore diversified research directions. Please feel free to contact me if you have relevant research opportunities or potential collaboration interests.
